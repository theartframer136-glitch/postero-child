The Art Framer Site Test

26 October 2026 theartframer.us as a 35-year-old shopper

The whole shop was read page by page, then walked through in a real browser the way a first-time buyer would: home, a category, a product, the cart, guest checkout to the payment step, then again with an account. This report lists what a shopper would run into, what caused it, what was fixed and what is left.

Summary

A shopper can find a piece, choose a size, add it to the cart and reach the payment step on desktop and on a phone without hitting a fault: the 45-step shopper journey passed with no failures. Three things a shopper could trip on were found around that path and are fixed in this branch: the home page's "Shop" link, spelt /Shop, opened the shop with no products on it; the "Sign Up" page had no form to sign up with; and the "Sign In" button on that page went nowhere. The rest is the host dropping requests under load (six tag pages, two category-page loads), three pairs of pieces sharing a name, and some search-engine hygiene.

3 high, 4 medium, 5 low findings and 4 notes. 4 are fixed in the code on branch claude/loving-allen-9owz8p, ready to merge and deploy; the rest are on the host, in Square, in Elementor, or decisions for the owner, and each says so.

What was changed in the code

  • Capital letters in an address redirect once (301) to the lower-case page, so /Shop no longer opens an empty shop. The query string keeps its case; 404s are untouched. inc/markup-fixes.php, part 14.
  • Empty "Sign In" / "Register" buttons on the login and sign-up pages are given their address as the finished page leaves the server. inc/markup-fixes.php, part 15.
  • Header links written as ?page_id= are replaced with the page's own address, so Sign In and Register open in one request instead of two. inc/markup-fixes.php, part 16.
  • The Sign Up page sends a signed-out visitor on to the My Account register form while WordPress registration is off (a 302, so nothing to undo if it is switched back on). inc/markup-fixes.php, part 17.
  • Both "View Product" buttons (the download modal and the try-on-wall tool) start with the shop address instead of none; their scripts still swap in the product when one is chosen. functions.php.
  • Two read-only probes, tools/probe-crawl-followups.mjs and tools/probe-browser-followups.mjs, so the follow-up tables can be re-run after any deploy.

Eleven unit checks cover the three new helpers and all pass; the two redirects are plain WordPress calls guarded by the page and the settings. The change is on the branch and not yet deployed: deploy happens when it is merged to main. After the deploy, run Audit Site with pass links and the two probes to confirm /Shop redirects, the Sign In buttons have an address and the header links are direct.

Left for the owner

  • Host capacity. The 508s and dropped connections are the shared plan's CPU cap. Two things help without a plan change: let LiteSpeed cache product-tag archives like categories, and keep the daily cache warm covering the tag pages shoppers reach from products. If 508s keep appearing in the host's logs, the plan is the fix.
  • Sign Up. Decide where "Sign Up" should lead for good: the My Account register form (then point the header link there and retire /sign-up/), or the Elementor widget's own register form, which needs WordPress registration switched back on.
  • Same-name pieces. Rename one of each of the three pairs so shoppers and Google can tell them apart.
  • Noindex categories. Decide whether Gifts, Home Decor and Gold Foiled & UV should rank; if so, give them products of their own.
  • Google Pay. Decide in Square whether the express wallet button should show on product pages; the console error goes with it either way.
  • Home page link. The /Shop link now redirects, but correcting it to /shop/ in the home page's Elementor content saves that hop too.
