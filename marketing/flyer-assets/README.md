# Flyer assets

Product photos and the gold AF logo, extracted from the Corporate Rate Card 2026
PDF so Canva can import them by URL for the corporate printing flyers.

Files are numbered by flyer: 00 is the cover, 01 to 32 follow the rate card
order. Numbers 11, 13, 15, 17, 26 and 31 have no file here. Their brochure
photos showed a different product or another business's branding, so those
flyers use a Canva-generated photo instead.
