The most common mistake with wall art in American homes is buying too small. US living rooms are large, sofas are 84 to 100 inches wide, and a 24-inch print above them looks like a postage stamp. This guide gives the sizes that work, wall by wall, for the deity and Vastu canvases most Indian-American families hang.

The two-thirds rule

Artwork above furniture should span two thirds to three quarters of the furniture's width. A 90-inch sofa wants 60 to 68 inches of art. A 60-inch console wants 40 to 45 inches. Below two thirds, the piece looks lost; wider than the furniture, it looks top-heavy.

Height: hang it at eye level

Galleries hang the centre of a piece at 57 to 60 inches from the floor. Above a sofa, leave 6 to 10 inches between the top of the sofa back and the bottom of the frame; above a console, 6 to 8 inches. American ceilings are often 9 or 10 feet, which tempts people to hang high. Resist: art hung too high reads as disconnected from the room.

Orientation

  • Landscape above sofas, beds and consoles.
  • Portrait for foyers, the wall beside a window, closet mandirs and narrow pooja corners.

Which size for which artwork

  • Seven horses: 3×4 ft or 3×5 ft landscape, living room or office. It is a statement piece and should be treated as one.
  • Radha Krishna: 3×4 ft above the sofa, 3×2 ft above a bedroom dresser.
  • Ganesha at the entrance: 2×3 ft portrait for an apartment, 2.5×3 ft for a house foyer.
  • Balaji, Shiva and other pooja-room images: 2×3 ft portrait in a closet mandir, 2.5×3 ft or 3×4 ft on a full wall.
  • Buddha: 3×4 ft in a living room, 2×3 ft in a meditation corner.

Floating frame or no frame

All our canvases come with a floating frame: a slim frame that sits a few millimetres away from the canvas edge, so the piece reads as gallery art rather than a stretched print. Black or dark walnut suits modern US interiors; gold suits traditional deity art and a pooja wall.

Measure before you order

Cut a sheet of newspaper or painter's paper to the canvas size and tape it to the wall for a day. If it looks right from the sofa and from the doorway, order that size. If in doubt between two sizes, choose the larger one.

Frequently asked questions

What size canvas goes above a queen bed?

A queen bed is 60 inches wide, so 3×2 ft landscape (36 inches) is the minimum and 3×4 ft fits well. Hang it 8 to 10 inches above the headboard, or on the facing wall for a deity image.

How heavy is a 3×4 ft framed canvas?

Around 8 to 12 pounds. Two picture hooks or a French cleat on drywall carry it safely.
