Radha Krishna is the one deity image Hindu tradition welcomes in the bedroom. Their love is read as the soul's longing for the divine, and as harmony between two people, which is why a Radha Krishna painting is the most common wedding gift for an Indian couple in the USA. Here is what the image means, where to hang it in the bedroom, and which version to choose.

The meaning

Radha represents pure devotion; Krishna represents divine consciousness. Together they stand for love that is complete, patient and spiritual rather than possessive. In a bedroom the image is a daily reminder of that kind of partnership. Krishna's flute, when it appears, is the call that draws the soul; the peacock feather is beauty and protection; the lotus is purity.

Where to hang it in the bedroom

  • Best wall: the wall you see from the bed, not the wall behind the headboard. Tradition prefers that you do not sleep with your feet pointing at a deity image and that the image is not directly above the sleeping couple.
  • Direction: north-east or east walls are preferred for any sacred image; in a bedroom, any wall other than the one behind the bed is acceptable.
  • Height: centre of the canvas at 57 to 60 inches from the floor, or a little higher if a dresser sits below it.
  • Avoid: the wall shared with a bathroom, directly facing a mirror, and the floor or a low shelf.

Which Radha Krishna for the bedroom

Choose a gentle composition: Radha and Krishna seated together, Krishna playing the flute to Radha, the couple on a swing or under a tree, with peacocks and lotus. Soft blues, pinks, creams and gold suit a bedroom; the very bright festival palettes are better in the living room. Avoid scenes of separation (viraha) for the bedroom; they are beautiful but tradition places them elsewhere.

Frequently asked questions

Is it all right to hang Radha Krishna above the bed?

Tradition prefers the wall you face from the bed rather than the headboard wall, so the image is seen and not slept under.

Can an unmarried person hang Radha Krishna in the bedroom?

Yes. The image stands for devotion and harmony, and many people hang it for peace and for the hope of a good partnership.

Which colours are best for a Radha Krishna painting in the bedroom?

Soft blues, pinks, creams and muted gold. Keep the brightest palettes for the living room.
