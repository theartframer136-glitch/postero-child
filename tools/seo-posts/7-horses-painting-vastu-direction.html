The seven running horses painting is the most searched Vastu artwork among Indian families in the USA, and the most often hung in the wrong place. This guide covers the direction, the wall, the room and the size, so the painting you bring home does the job Vastu tradition gives it.

What the seven horses stand for

In Vastu Shastra and in Feng Shui, seven horses galloping together represent speed, strength, unity and forward momentum. The number seven ties to the seven rays of the sun and the seven horses that pull Surya's chariot in Hindu tradition. Together the symbol is read as success, career growth and financial progress that keeps moving, never stuck.

That is why the painting turns up in living rooms, home offices, reception areas and boardrooms across Indian-American homes and businesses: it is a daily reminder to keep going, and a traditional invitation for prosperity to follow.

The right direction: south or east

Vastu guidance is consistent on two walls:

  • South wall: the direction of fame, recognition and success. The classic placement for a living room or office.
  • East wall: the direction of the rising sun, new beginnings and growth. A good choice for a home office or study.

Some practitioners also accept the north wall, the direction of wealth, especially when the horses are painted in sunrise tones. Avoid the west wall, and never hang the painting facing a door or a window.

Which way the horses should run

This matters as much as the wall. The horses must gallop into the room, towards the inside of your home, never towards the exit. Energy, in the Vastu reading, follows the horses; running out of the door, it leaves the house. Check the artwork before you buy: every horse should face forward, head up, legs in motion, none looking back or appearing tired.

Rooms to use and rooms to avoid

  • Living room: the best place. A statement wall behind the sofa or opposite the main seating is ideal.
  • Home office or study: strongly recommended, on the wall you face while working.
  • Entrance foyer: fine, on the wall inside the door, with the horses running inward.
  • Bedroom: avoid. The painting carries motion and drive, which Vastu keeps out of the room meant for rest.
  • Pooja room: avoid. The mandir wall belongs to the deities.
  • Bathroom, kitchen, staircase wall: avoid.

Colour and background

White horses are the traditional favourite for the home, read as purity and calm strength. Golden, brown or sunrise-coloured horses suit offices and east walls. The background should be open: a rising sun, open land, calm water. Avoid dark, stormy or chaotic backgrounds, and avoid any painting where the horses are tied, saddled or standing still.

Size for an American wall

US living rooms are larger than most Indian ones, and a small print disappears above a 90-inch sofa. A good rule: the artwork should span two thirds of the furniture below it. For a standard sofa that means a 3×4 ft canvas; for a sectional or a long office credenza, 3×5 ft. Hang the centre of the painting at about 57 to 60 inches from the floor, the height galleries use.

Frequently asked questions

Can I hang a 7 horses painting in the bedroom?

Vastu tradition says no. The bedroom is for rest; the horses carry motion and ambition. Keep the painting in the living room or office.

Should the horses face left or right?

Neither matters on its own. What matters is that they run into the room, away from the door, and that every horse faces forward.

Is a seven horses painting a good housewarming gift?

It is one of the most popular Griha Pravesh gifts among Indian families in the USA, precisely because it is a wish for the new home's success. Pair it with a note on where to hang it.
