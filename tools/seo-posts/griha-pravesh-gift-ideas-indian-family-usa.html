A Griha Pravesh in the USA usually means a new suburban house or a first apartment, a priest on video call or in person, and a stream of friends arriving with gifts. The traditional gifts still apply, and the best of them is art for the new walls, because a new home has more empty walls than anything else. Here are the gifts that are right by tradition and right for an American house.

The traditional first three gifts

  1. Ganesha for the entrance. The remover of obstacles greets everyone who enters. A 2×3 ft or 2.5×3 ft Ganesha canvas is the first piece most families hang in a new home.
  2. Lakshmi Ganesh for the pooja space. Wealth and wisdom together, blessing the household from day one.
  3. Seven running horses for the living room or office. The Vastu wish for success and momentum in the new chapter. See the Seven Horses collection.

What makes canvas art the practical gift

  • It arrives framed and ready to hang, no framing shop needed in a new town.
  • It ships to any US address, so you can send it even when you cannot attend.
  • It fits the budget: around $60 for a 2×3 ft piece, around $80 for 3×4 ft, around $100 for 3×5 ft, in US dollars.
  • It outlasts sweets, flowers and gift cards, and the family sees it every day.

Timing

Canvases are made in India and shipped across the USA in 7 to 14 days, with the shipping cost shown at checkout. Order as soon as you have the date; three weeks ahead is safe. If the ceremony is sooner, send a card with the artwork's picture and let the canvas follow.

Gifts to avoid for a Griha Pravesh

Tradition avoids gifting sharp objects, clocks, black items and anything used. Among artworks, avoid images of deities in fierce forms, scenes of war or sorrow, and sinking ships or lone animals; all are read as the wrong wish for a new home. Keep to deities in blessing poses, Vastu symbols of growth, and calm nature.

A note on size for American homes

New US homes have tall walls and wide sofas. For the living room choose 3×4 ft; for a foyer or a pooja corner, 2×3 ft portrait. If you do not know the layout yet, 2.5×3 ft is the size that fits almost anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

Is it all right to gift a deity image to someone else's home?

Yes, it is one of the most traditional housewarming gifts. Choose the deity the family worships, or Ganesha when you are not sure.

Can I have the gift delivered on the day of the ceremony?

Deliveries cannot be timed to the day, so send it a week early and ask the family to open it at the ceremony.

What is the single safest Griha Pravesh artwork?

Ganesha. Every Hindu home begins with him, and every size and style suits an entrance.
