Diwali gifting in the USA has a problem: the sweets arrive stale, the gold is out of budget, and nobody needs a fourth set of diyas. A framed deity or Vastu canvas solves it. It is personal, it is traditional, it lasts for years on the wall, and it ships to any state. This guide matches the right artwork to the right person, with every piece in US dollars.

For a new home: Lakshmi Ganesh

The classic Diwali image and the traditional Griha Pravesh gift in one. Lakshmi for prosperity, Ganesha for a smooth beginning. A 2×3 ft canvas suits an apartment pooja corner; 3×2 ft landscape suits a console in a house. See the Lakshmi Ganesha collection.

For parents or in-laws: Tirupati Balaji, Ram Darbar, Shiva

Older relatives usually have a clear ishta devata, so match it. Tirupati Balaji for Telugu and Tamil families, Ram Darbar for North Indian households, a meditating Shiva for a devotee of Mahadev. Choose a serene, traditional rendering rather than a modern abstract for this generation.

For a newly married couple: Radha Krishna

Divine love, harmony and togetherness. A Radha Krishna canvas in soft tones for the bedroom or a vibrant one for the living room is the most gifted piece at Indian-American weddings and first Diwalis together. 3×4 ft makes a statement above a sofa.

For someone starting a business or a new job: Seven Horses

The seven running horses is the Vastu gift for ambition: success, speed and momentum, meant for the office wall or the living room. It is also the one gift that works for a colleague or client who is not Hindu, since its meaning is read as motivation and progress across cultures.

For a friend who meditates or does yoga: Buddha

A serene Buddha with lotus suits a meditation corner, a yoga room or a calm bedroom. It is the safe choice when you do not know the recipient's tradition.

For the host of the Diwali party: Pichwai or Indian heritage art

A Pichwai of Shrinathji with cows and lotus, or a classical dancer from the Indian Culture collection, is decorative and festive, perfect for a dining or hallway wall.

Timing: order by these dates

Diwali falls in late October or early November. Allow the full 14 days plus a margin: order at least three weeks before Diwali for a gift that must be on the wall before the lamps are lit. For Dhanteras, the day the Lakshmi image is traditionally brought home, order a week earlier still.

How to present it

Ship it directly to the recipient with a gift note, or carry it to the party: a framed canvas up to 3×4 ft fits across the back seat of a car. Tell the recipient where to hang it. For Lakshmi Ganesh, the pooja space or the entrance; for the seven horses, the south or east wall of the living room or office; for Radha Krishna, the living room or bedroom.

Frequently asked questions

Can you ship a Diwali gift straight to the recipient?

Yes. Enter their US address at checkout. Every canvas ships framed and ready to hang.

What if I am late and Diwali is next week?

Choose a digital download of the artwork and have a local print shop print it, or order the canvas and tell the recipient it is on its way: a late Diwali gift is still a Diwali gift.

Is a deity painting an appropriate gift for a non-Hindu friend or colleague?

Choose the seven horses, a Buddha or an Indian heritage piece rather than a deity. All three are read as art and meaning rather than worship.
